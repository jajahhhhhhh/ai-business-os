# Runbook — Local SEO & Google Business Profile (howtoniksen.com)

For a physical boutique villa on Koh Samui, the **local map pack** (and Google
Business Profile behind it) is the fastest realistic first-page win — it sits
above the organic blue links, and OTAs don't dominate it the way they dominate
organic. This runbook is the do-it-yourself plan; the marketing agents (Social,
Analytics, Email/LINE) feed and measure it but do not replace the manual
account setup.

Context: howtoniksen.com starts at Ahrefs Domain Rating **0.0** (measured), so
organic ranking for head terms is a months-long effort — local/GBP is the near-
term lever.

## ⚠️ Eligibility & policy (read first)

A Google Business Profile is allowed for a vacation/boutique villa **only if
guests physically stay at a real address**, and it is **one listing per
location**. Fast ways to get suspended (months of setback):

- Fake or incentivised reviews.
- Keyword-stuffing the business name — use `How to Niksen`, NOT
  `How to Niksen Boutique Pool Villa Koh Samui`.
- Duplicate listings for one villa.
- Inaccurate address/hours.

## Phase 1 — Claim & verify (week 1)

1. Claim the profile at business.google.com.
2. **Name:** exactly the brand — `How to Niksen`.
3. **Address:** the real villa address. Thailand verification is usually
   **video verification** — film exterior + a nearby landmark + interior in one
   continuous take.
4. **Primary category:** `Vacation home rental` (or `Holiday home` /
   `Resort hotel` if a better fit). **Secondary:** `Villa`, `Guest house`.
5. **Phone:** a Thai number that is actually answered. **Website:**
   howtoniksen.com. **Hours:** check-in/out policy or 24h.

## Phase 2 — Complete the profile (week 1–2)

Completeness is a direct ranking factor — fill everything, in Thai + English:

- **Description** (~750 chars): brand voice + niksen/slow-living angle, naturally
  including "private pool villa", "[area] Koh Samui", "quiet retreat". No
  keyword stuffing.
- **Photos:** 20+ high-quality (pool, bedrooms, view, exterior, neighbourhood);
  add new ones monthly. Name files descriptively before upload
  (`private-pool-villa-[area]-koh-samui.jpg`).
- **Attributes:** Wi-Fi, pool, air-con, parking, pet policy, accessibility — tick
  all that apply.
- **Booking link** → the direct-booking page (keeps commission off OTAs and
  drives traffic to the DR-0 site).

## Phase 3 — Reviews engine (ongoing — the biggest local lever)

- **Ask every guest at checkout** via a GBP review short-link on a card / auto-
  sent through LINE or email (automatable by the OS Email/LINE flow).
- Steady **velocity** (a few per month), never a burst.
- **Respond to every review** within 48h, in the reviewer's language, on-brand.
- **Never buy or incentivise** reviews — detected and penalised.

## Phase 4 — Citations & NAP consistency (week 2–4)

Name / Address / Phone must be **byte-identical** everywhere. Build/verify:

- TripAdvisor, Agoda, Booking.com, Airbnb (also rank for the brand + build
  trust).
- Thailand + Samui local directories; Bing Places; Apple Maps.

Pick one canonical NAP format ("Road" vs "Rd.", one phone format) and enforce it
— inconsistency actively hurts.

## Phase 5 — Ongoing, wired into the OS

- **Google Posts weekly** — fed by the Social agent's content calendar (offers,
  "things to do near [area]", seasonal availability).
- **Seed Q&A** on the profile (ask + answer: airport transfer? minimum stay?
  pet-friendly?).
- **On-site local content + schema:** add a `LodgingBusiness` structured-data
  block with geo-coordinates to howtoniksen.com; add location pages
  ("Private pool villa in [area], Koh Samui").
- **Measure monthly** via GBP Insights (Views, Direction requests, Calls,
  Website clicks) and track the map-pack rank for "villa [area] koh samui" —
  slots into the Analytics agent's reporting.

## First-week checklist

```
☐ Claim GBP for "How to Niksen"
☐ Complete video verification
☐ Primary category = Vacation home rental (+ secondaries)
☐ 20+ photos, full description, attributes, booking link, Thai + English
☐ Generate the review short-link; add it to the checkout LINE/email flow
☐ Confirm identical NAP on TripAdvisor / Agoda / Booking / Airbnb
```

**Realistic outcome:** the map pack for hyper-local terms ("villa [area] koh
samui", "private pool villa near [landmark]") is winnable in **weeks** — well
before organic blue-link rankings move.
